# Vertical slice brief (draft 0)

Status: **not approved**.  Owned by the `game-designer` agent.

The slice proves the content pipeline and one gameplay beat, not the whole hack.

## Scope

- 3–5 rooms, Test Area slots or one remixed sector entrance.
- 1 enemy variant or one new hazard.
- 1 event/text beat.
- 1 pickup (ability, tank, or key item).
- 1 door loop: enter → fight/trigger → reward → exit.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Boot → warp/enter the slice → complete the beat → leave.
- Save → load → the slice state persists.
- No crash, no softlock, no OAM overflow, no new lag frames beyond budget.
- Vanilla rooms outside the slice behave unchanged (differential smoke).
